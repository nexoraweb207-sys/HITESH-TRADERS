Sanganeri Print Care Guide

Follow these heritage care practices to maintain print vibrancy and fabric softness for decades.

Cold Gentle Wash

Machine wash in cold water on gentle cycle with liquid detergent to protect handprinted natural dyes.

Wash Separately

Wash dark indigo and madder reds separately during initial washes to ensure color setting.

Dry in Shade

Line dry inside-out under shade to retain natural cotton luster and avoid harsh sun fading.

Medium Iron

Iron on medium-warm heat setting for a crisp, crisp, royal hotel bed setting feel.
